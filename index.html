About Me

Daud Khan is a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Fellow on the ANCHOR project (Advanced Network Connectivity Using Harmonious Optical and Radio Technologies) at Instituto de Telecomunicações. He is a graduate researcher in Electrical and Computer Engineering specializing in autonomous driving, cooperative perception, and intelligent wireless communication systems.

He served as a Graduate Research Assistant at the Mobile Telecommunication Research Lab, Inha University, South Korea, and previously worked in the Digital Signal Processing Lab at UET Peshawar. His research spans Level 4 autonomy, V2X Uu/PC5 interfaces, mobility and handover management, non-terrestrial networks, AI-RAN, semantic communications, and AI-native 6G systems.

He has authored and co-authored SCI-indexed journal articles and conference papers, contributed to three filed Korean patents and two registered software programs, participated in Korean government-funded research projects, and serves as a peer reviewer for IEEE Access.
