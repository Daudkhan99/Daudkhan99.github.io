About Me

Daud Khan is a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Fellow at Instituto de Telecomunicações and a PhD candidate in Electrical Engineering at the University of Aveiro, Portugal. His current research focuses on radio-stripe-enabled cell-free massive MIMO systems designed to provide wide-area coverage and reliable connectivity in high-mobility environments.

Previously, he served as a Graduate Research Assistant at Inha University, South Korea, where he earned his Master’s degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering. During his studies, he received the BK21 (Brain Korea 21) Scholarship from the Government of South Korea and the Jungseok International Scholarship from Inha University. He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Communication Engineering from the University of Engineering and Technology (UET) Peshawar, Pakistan.

He has authored and co-authored several SCI-indexed journal articles and conference papers and contributed to patented technologies in autonomous driving and wireless communications. He has also participated in Korean government-funded research projects and serves as a peer reviewer for IEEE Access.
